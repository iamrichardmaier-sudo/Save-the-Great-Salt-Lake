## What it takes to hit the goal

A search over every policy dial, within its existing range, found the new default package:
- a mandate at its maximum: a 1,200 KAF floor phased in over one year;
- the crop levy at its maximum, $400/AF above 1.5 AF an acre;
- repossession switched on;
- monitoring at $50M and phragmites removal at $25M.

It's fragile. Any one of these misses the goal:
- a mandate 10% weaker;
- no levy, or no repossession;
- the slow end of the lake's response;
- a drier inflow baseline.

More money barely helps: $200M a year only reaches 4,198.69 ft. The mandate, levy and repossession would all need new legislation.
